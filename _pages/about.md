Hi, I am Shamse. I am currently a Ph.D. Student in the Department of Computer Science at the <a href='#'>University of Saskatchewan</a>, working under the supervision of <a href='#'>Dr. Banani Roy</a>. My research sits at the intersection of software engineering and human-AI collaboration. As autonomous agents evolve from simple tools into active teammates, my research investigates how this shift impacts developer workflows and community dynamics. Currently, my work focuses on role-calibrated human-agent teaming. I empirically study how interactions with AI agents differ across the social hierarchy of software projects. By analyzing the behaviors of both core and peripheral developers, I aim to design more adaptive, role-aware AI systems that foster trust, reduce friction, and improve code quality in open-source and enterprise environments. Alongside this, I explore how explanation styles in AI-assisted code review can be personalized to individual developer profiles. My broader research portfolio includes studies on gender diversity and code quality in AI systems and socio-technical debt in machine learning projects. Together, these threads drive toward a unified goal: designing adaptive and inclusive AI systems that foster trust, reduce friction, and support developers as individuals, not just as an undifferentiated mass.
